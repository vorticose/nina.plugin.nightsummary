Observations with Dual-Axis + Per-Filter Coloring

  • ADU separation: L's high ADU makes it instantly visually distinct on the right axis even in interleaved sequences — you can see the L cluster high and RGB cluster low
  • Block (A): Cleanest — each curve is a solid line, ADU step-down at filter switches is a clear visual signature
  • Ratio cycling (B): Readable — L curves are denser, sparse R/G/B still track the trend
  • Pure interleave (C): ADU zigzags sharply between L and RGB levels every frame — right axis becomes very noisy
  • Shared right axis: When L ADU (~15–18k) and RGB ADU (~3–5k) share one scale, the RGB variation is compressed into a narrow band at the bottom of the right axis, making fine RGB ADU trends hard to see — the chip selector avoids this by rescaling per filter
  • Narrowband: Works well — ADU values are similar across filters and HFR tracks closely, so two clean parallel curves emerge
